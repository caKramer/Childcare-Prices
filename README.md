# Childcare-Prices
## Storytelling with Data - based on Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic's guidance

Reviewing childcare prices with the National Database of Childcare Prices dataset. 

Contains Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and visualizations.

**Data changes:** 

- dropping rows that were missing values
  
In the early years of tracking, 2008, less than half, 45.3% of surveyed counties reported a complete profile across every single age bracket. By 2016 it peaked to 84.1% complete before stabilizing in the mid to high 70s for 2017 and 2018.

Presentation focused with three different visualizations to communicate findings to viewers

**Target Audiences**

- U.S wide focus where Health and Human Services (HHS) and other federal government agencies could affect change for childcare prices
- State level and is centered around Nebraska Governor, State Senators, Representatives, and Nebraska Counties Chamber of Commerce
- General Public, specifically focused for local Nebraskans in the Omaha/Lincoln metro area

**Mediums**

- PowerPoint
- Dashboard
- Infographic
